How to read it

  1. Header tells you whether to trust what you see: when it last ran and whether the data feed is healthy.
  2. At a glance is the answer to "do I need to read anything today?" in three numbers.
  3. Filter bar keeps it usable once there are dozens of findings.
  4. Feed is the main reading area, ranked by urgency, with quiet holdings collapsed so they do not add noise.
  5. Detail panel shows the reasoning and sources for whichever finding is selected, with no buy or sell advice.
  6. Watchlist triage keeps the 35 cheaper-checked names in one table, so you can see what got escalated and why.
  7. Run history is for debugging, not reading.
